Untitled Note

A simple, legibility-focused environment for reading and drafting text.

First Thoughts

This layout prioritizes high contrast, generous whitespace, and zero visual distractions. By relying solely on natural typography scales and subtle grey tones, the content remains the primary focus.

Text wraps dynamically with balanced line lengths to ensure comfortable continuous reading across mobile and desktop screens.

Structure and Content

Documents can be organized using standard heading structures, plain text paragraphs, blockquotes, or lists.

Key Principles

  • Monotone palette with no accent highlights
  • Custom lemon-yellow text selection
  • Responsive typography powered by Pretendard Variable
  • Minimalist borders separating major sections
"Simplicity is about subtracting the obvious and adding the meaningful."

You can seamlessly draft ideas, store quick notes, or publish long-form essays without altering the underlying markup.
